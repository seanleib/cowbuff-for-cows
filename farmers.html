🐮 Cowbuff for Cows

A calm cartoon made for cows by Hudson. 7 seasons, 119 episodes, each 5 minutes. Cowbuff is a yellow cow who eats grass, naps, meets friends, and speaks in slow, low moos. There is no English and nothing scary or loud.

Be honest: we are testing it

Nobody knows yet if cows understand or enjoy a screen show. Cows cannot tell us, so please treat this as a small, gentle trial. The cows decide. If they do not like it, we stop.

How to try it (5 minutes)

  1. Open player.html on a screen or tablet at a safe spot where cows rest (out of reach, so they cannot knock it over).
  2. Pick a quiet time, when cows are calm and resting (not during feeding rush or milking at first).
  3. Start with volume low (about as loud as a quiet barn). Press Quieter if in doubt.
  4. Play one episode only: Season 1, Episode 1 (“Good Morning, Meadow”). It is very slow on purpose.
  5. Stand back and watch. Do not make the cows stay.

What to watch for

Good signs: keeps chewing the cud, lies down calmly, ears relaxed, looks toward the screen and stays, comes closer slowly, soft low moo back.

Stop right away if: they walk away, bunch up, ears pinned back, wide eyes, tail swishing a lot, stop eating or chewing, loud or worried mooing. Press “Cows do not like it - stop”. The player remembers that it was stopped, so we can try quieter, shorter, or another time.

Why it is made this way

  • Low, slow moos, not human talking. Cows make low sounds to stay in touch with each other.
  • Gentle background hum with no sudden noises or loud bangs.
  • Slow, soft movement in greens, yellows and blues, which cows see best (cows do not see red well).
  • Only 5 minutes per episode, so a trial is short.
